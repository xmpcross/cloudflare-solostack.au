# Surface brief: homepage (Persuade)

Scope: solostack.au homepage and the shared tokens, header and footer it sets. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Australian solo operators first, developer-founders included. Job: pick a stack, tool or gear item and follow an affiliate link with confidence. Proof: real stacks, tools, gear and posts from the database, A$ prices, each tool's recorded pro and con, post photography, brand marks, two AI-generated images labelled as such. Constraints: static export, 1366px content width, en-AU, text weights capped at 700, no unbacked claims, affiliate disclosure visible on the cover. Must show a business and services section.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SoloStack is a field guide to the one-person business, and every tool, stack and piece of gear is a catalogued specimen plate. It refuses the indigo SaaS card grid with a pill row and a stack of identical sections.
OWN-WORLD: Bass Strait sea-green carrying the cover, salt-white plates with a fine double frame, deep marine ink for the software catalogue and the footer, and one coral action colour that also drenches the business section. Urbanist display at 700 and 600, Figtree text, uppercase tracked captions. Plates carry figure numbers and a caption of name, tagline, A$ price and verdict. Drawn eucalyptus sprigs, one drawn mark per gear kind, and a framed woodblock print are the recurring devices. Recognisable with all content removed by the sea-green field, framed salt plates and uppercase Fig. captions.
STORY: A visitor understands in seconds that this is a priced, catalogued guide for one-person businesses; believes it because real prices, a Good and Watch line per tool, a commission note and a named operator sit beside every plate; and opens the Online Course Creator stack or searches a guide.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sea-green field. Left 6 columns: Urbanist H1, one sentence, the search, and a coral button to the stack. Right 6 columns: Plate 1, a salt framed sheet showing the stack as four figures (brand mark, figure number, name, tagline, A$ price) under the title and the ≈A$151/mo total, a framed print pinned to its corner, and under it a one-line commission note with a How we choose link.
ORDER: cover (sea), Software priced (ink), Field notes (salt), Gear picked (salt-2), the business and service colophon (coral), then What you can use and the disclosure on one calm salt field.
FORM: Field guide with specimen plates, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 40114356. Raise from Saul Bass: one idea per saturated field, so sections swap the field colour as the visitor scrolls.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
