# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Australian solo operators (freelancers, creators, one-person businesses) choosing software, hardware and stacks. They arrive to compare options and decide what to buy or subscribe to.

## Product Purpose
SoloStack (solostack.au) is an editorial affiliate site: guides, tool reviews, hardware picks, comparisons and stack blueprints for one-person businesses in Australia. Success is a visitor opening a stack, comparison or guide and following an affiliate link with confidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static export on Cloudflare Pages; content is a snapshot from the Payload database (categories, posts, stacks, tools, hardware, comparisons, coupons).
- Home page data: categories, 100 newest posts, up to 10 stacks, tools and hardware.
- Fonts: Urbanist for display and headings and Figtree for text, chosen by the owner on 24 Oct 2026 for the redesign (replacing Young Serif, Hanken Grotesk and Funnel trials; the owner asked for a geometric sans like Urbanist or Outfit); applied on the homepage first, rolling out to inner pages (which still use Manrope and Mulish until then). Weights capped at 700.
- Content max width 1366px, centred (organisation default).
- Locale en-AU. Affiliate disclosure page exists.
- Newsletter: no provider is connected; the current form only shows a browser alert.

## Evidence on Hand
Real content only from the database and /media uploads, plus brand logos in public/brand-logos. The current hero metrics ("100% Verified", "$50k+ Async Goal"), the hard-coded "$149/mo" blueprint card and tool prices on the home page are not backed by data and must not be reused as claims.

## Product Principles
- Say only what the data supports; show real stacks, tools and posts.
- Help a buyer decide fast: price, verdict and next step visible without hunting.
- Australian context first (AUD, local retailers and hosts).
- Disclose affiliate relationships plainly.
